HOW TO PUT THE INDEPENDENT EDUCATION WEBSITE ONLINE
====================================================

1. Buy a domain (e.g. independenteducation.com.bd or .com) and hosting
   (any cPanel hosting with PHP 8 works).
2. Upload EVERYTHING in this folder to public_html, keeping the img folder
   (EXCEPT the n8n-automation-DO-NOT-UPLOAD folder - DO NOT UPLOAD THE n8n
   files to your website; import them into n8n instead):
      index.html, .htaccess, favicon.ico, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, img/,
      server-tracking/
   (.htaccess starts with a dot and may be hidden - turn on "show hidden
   files" in cPanel File Manager. It adds compression, caching and HTTPS.)
3. Replace "www.your-domain.com" in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your domain.
4. In index.html, near the top, add your canonical link (see the comment there),
   and change og:image to the full address, e.g.
      https://www.YOUR-DOMAIN/img/og-share.jpg
5. Turn on free HTTPS (SSL) in your hosting control panel.
6. Submit your site in Google Search Console and add sitemap.xml there.
7. Optional: tracking and enquiry capture - see MARKETING-TOOLS.txt, server-tracking/META-TOOLS-PLAYBOOK.txt (all 35 Meta tools), server-tracking/META-SETUP.txt, server-tracking/WHATSAPP-SETUP.txt, server-tracking/WHATSAPP-TOOLS.txt, server-tracking/INSTAGRAM-TOOLS.txt, server-tracking/LEAD-CAPTURE-TOOLS.txt, server-tracking/GOOGLE-TOOLS-PLAYBOOK.txt (all 52 Google tools), server-tracking/GOOGLE-SETUP.txt, server-tracking/LINKEDIN-SETUP.txt, server-tracking/TIKTOK-SETUP.txt, n8n-automation-DO-NOT-UPLOAD/N8N-SETUP.txt,
   server-tracking/PIXEL-SETUP.txt and README.txt.
   (server-tracking/track.php goes in public_html too; keep the config file
   above public_html.)

Note: the "Ask our AI assistant" chat only works inside the Claude preview.
On your own hosting it hides itself automatically; the WhatsApp and
Messenger chat buttons keep working.

APP AND OFFLINE FILES (added November 2026)
- Upload sw.js and offline.html to the same top folder as index.html.
- They make the site installable as an app ("Install our app" button in the footer,
  shown only in browsers that support it) and keep it working on a weak connection.
- They only start working on the real website over https, not in a preview.
- After you change the website later, open sw.js and change VERSION = 'ie-v1'
  to 'ie-v2' (and so on) so returning visitors get the new version.

SEO FILES (added November 2026)
- Upload robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt and the IndexNow key file (the .txt
  file with a long random name) to the same top folder as index.html.
- Before uploading, replace www.your-domain.com with your real domain in
  index.html, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt.
- SEO-GUIDE.txt explains Google Search Console, Bing, Google Business Profile,
  keywords and content. You don't need to upload it (if you do, .htaccess
  blocks it from the public).

After receiving an offer

After receiving an offer

You have an offer. What's next?

Receiving an offer is a big milestone. Now it is time to decide, secure your place and prepare for your visa and departure. Our counsellors can help you weigh up your offers and plan the next steps.

Get help with my offer

Next steps after receiving an offer

Track your application

Keep an eye on each university portal and your email. Note whether each offer is conditional or unconditional, and which conditions you still need to meet, such as final results or an English score.

  • Check portals and email regularly
  • List outstanding conditions and their deadlines
  • Send missing documents quickly

Compare your offers

If you have more than one offer, compare them side by side before you accept. Once you accept, you may need to decline the others.

  • Total tuition fees and living costs
  • Scholarships and fee discounts
  • Course content, location and work-placement options
  • Acceptance and deposit deadlines

Accept and pay your fees

Accept your chosen offer by the deadline and pay any required deposit or first-semester tuition. The university then issues the document you need for your visa.

  • Pay only through official university channels
  • Keep every payment receipt
  • Get your CoE, CAS, I-20 or enrolment letter

Arrange student essentials

Before your visa application and departure, sort out the practical things you will need as an international student.

  • Education loan or bank sponsorship, if needed
  • Student health insurance (often a visa requirement)
  • International money transfer and a bank account
  • Accommodation for when you arrive

Protect yourself from fraud

Always pay tuition fees directly to the university's official bank account or payment portal, and check the details on the university's own website. Never send fees to a personal account.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if my visa is refused?

Read the refusal letter carefully to understand the reasons. Depending on the country, you may be able to reapply with stronger documents or request a review. Talk to a counsellor before you reapply.

Can I reapply after declining an offer?

Usually yes, but you would normally need to submit a new application for a later intake. Check the university's policy before you decline.

When should I buy health insurance?

Many countries require proof of student health cover as part of the visa application, so arrange it after accepting your offer and before you apply for your visa.

Can I talk to students already studying there?

Many universities run student ambassador programmes, online chats and social media groups. Ask us and we'll help you find ways to connect.

What if I am not accepted?

Don't give up. Ask the university for feedback, consider pathway or foundation programmes, improve your English score, or apply to other universities that fit your profile.

Not sure which offer to accept?

Bring your offer letters to a free counselling session and we'll compare them with you.

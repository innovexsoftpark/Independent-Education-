INDEPENDENT EDUCATION - GOOGLE TRACKING, LEADS AND VISIBILITY CHECKLIST (November 2026)
=====================================================================================

Tick each box as you finish it. Google changes its menus often; if a step
does not match what you see, follow Google's own help pages.


01  COMPLETE GOOGLE TRACKING
----------------------------
[ ] 1. Install GA4
       analytics.google.com > Admin > Create property > Web stream.
       Copy the Measurement ID (G-XXXXXXXXXX) and paste it into index.html:
         GA4_ID: "G-XXXXXXXXXX"      (search for "const IE_MARKETING")
       GA4 loads only after a visitor clicks "Accept" on the cookie notice.
       Full steps: server-tracking/GOOGLE-SETUP.txt

[ ] 2. Verify Google Search Console
       search.google.com/search-console > Add property > "URL prefix" >
       "HTML tag". Copy only the content="..." value and paste it into:
         GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION: "abc123..."
       Upload the site, then click Verify.
       Then: Sitemaps > submit  https://www.your-domain.com/sitemap.xml
       (the sitemap now also lists the 4 guide pages).
       Link Search Console to GA4: GA4 Admin > Product links > Search Console.

[ ] 3. Mark lead conversions as KEY EVENTS in GA4
       GA4 > Admin > Data display > Events (or "Key events"). Events appear
       there about 24 hours after they first happen, so send one test enquiry
       first. Then switch on "Mark as key event" for:

         generate_lead        every enquiry: contact form, Free Quote form,
                              chat-box form (form name is in "content_name":
                              "Contact form", "Free quote form", ...)
         book_counselling     the counselling booking form in the chat box
         book_appointment     click on your online booking page (BOOKING_URL)
         contact_whatsapp     WhatsApp clicks (optional: many clicks are not
                              real enquiries; you can keep it as a normal event)
         qualify_lead         sent by convert.php when the CRM marks a lead qualified
         close_convert_lead   sent by convert.php when a student enrols

       Optional: Admin > Custom definitions > create event-scoped dimensions
       "content_name", "destination" and "study_level" to see which form and
       which country bring the most leads.

[ ] 4. Google Ads (only if you run ads)
       Google Ads > Goals > Conversions > Import > Google Analytics 4 >
       choose generate_lead (and qualify_lead). Or use GOOGLE_ADS_ID and
       GOOGLE_ADS_LEAD_LABEL in IE_MARKETING (GOOGLE-SETUP.txt).

[ ] 5. Test
       GA4 > Admin > DebugView, or Reports > Realtime. Accept cookies on the
       site, send a test Free Quote, and check that generate_lead appears.
       Never put names, phone numbers or emails into GA4: the website already
       keeps them out of GA4 events.

BUILD QUALITY BACKLINKS (done outside the website; no code can do this)
    Good links come from real, relevant websites. Start with:
[ ] Google Business Profile: complete it fully, same name/address/phone as
    the site, add photos, ask real students for reviews (never buy reviews
    or write them yourself).
[ ] Universities you represent: ask to be listed on their "find an agent /
    representative" page with a link to your website.
[ ] Professional bodies or training you hold (for example British Council
    agent training, ICEF, PIER/QEAC): many list certified agents with links.
    Only claim memberships you actually have.
[ ] Bangladeshi business directories and the local chamber of commerce.
[ ] Education fairs and seminars you run or join: ask the organiser to link
    to your event page.
[ ] Guest articles or interviews in Bangladeshi newspapers' education
    sections, student blogs and alumni groups (useful content, not adverts).
[ ] Your social profiles (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube):
    put the website link in each bio.
    AVOID: buying links, link farms, "1000 backlinks" packages, and swapping
    links with unrelated sites. Google can lower your ranking for these.
    Track links in Search Console > Links, and in Semrush (SEO-GUIDE.txt B10).

MONTHLY CHECK (15 minutes)
    Search Console: clicks, top search queries, pages with errors.
    GA4: users by source, generate_lead by source and form, qualify_lead
    and close_convert_lead (which channels bring enrolled students).

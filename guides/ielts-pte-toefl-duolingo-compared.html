IELTS vs PTE vs TOEFL vs Duolingo: which English test should you take?

By the Independent Education counselling team · Updated 6 November 2026

All four tests can open doors, but not every university and not every visa accepts every test. The best test for you is the one your chosen universities and your visa accept, in a format you are comfortable with.

Always check acceptance first. Look at the English requirements page of each university you apply to, and the visa rules of the country. Some visas only accept certain tests or certain versions (for example the UK has its own list of Secure English Language Tests for some visa routes).

At a glance

TestScore scaleFormatRun by
IELTS AcademicBand 0–9 (half bands)Paper or computer at a test centre; speaking face-to-face with an examiner. An online version also exists, but it is not accepted everywhere.British Council, IDP, Cambridge
PTE Academic10–90Computer-based at a test centre, scored by computerPearson
TOEFL iBTFrom 21 January 2026: 1–6 (half steps), aligned to CEFR. For two years, score reports also show a comparable 0–120 score.Computer-based, at a centre or at homeETS
Duolingo English Test10–160Online at home, about one hourDuolingo

Because TOEFL changed its scale in 2026, some universities show the old 0–120 score, some the new 1–6 score and some both. If you are unsure, ask the admissions office which number they need.

How to choose

Choose IELTS if…

you want the most widely recognised option, or you prefer speaking with a real person. IELTS is accepted for most UK, Australian, Canadian and New Zealand universities and many visa routes. Check whether you need "IELTS for UKVI" for a UK visa route.

Choose PTE Academic if…

you are comfortable with computer tests and want fast results. It is widely accepted in Australia and New Zealand and by many UK universities. Check acceptance for Canada and the USA course by course.

Choose TOEFL iBT if…

you are applying mainly to the USA, where it is very widely accepted. Also used in Europe and Asia.

Choose Duolingo if…

your universities accept it and you want a cheaper, at-home option. Many US and some UK and European universities accept it, but it is often not accepted for visa purposes on its own, and acceptance changes. Check each university.

Converting scores between tests

Test providers publish their own concordance tables, for example Pearson's PTE concordance (on the PTE website) and ETS's TOEFL score comparison. Universities do not always follow these tables; their own requirement page decides.

Preparing well

  • Take a free official practice test first to see your starting level.
  • Learn the format: timing matters as much as English level.
  • Practise speaking and writing with feedback from a trained teacher.
  • Book your test date with enough time to retake before deadlines.

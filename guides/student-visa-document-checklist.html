Student visa document checklist for Bangladeshi students

By the Independent Education counselling team · Updated 6 November 2026

Most refused visas we see are not about bad results. They are about missing, unclear or inconsistent documents. Use this list to get organised early, then check the exact list for your case on the official page.

Important: this is a general checklist, not legal advice. Requirements depend on your course, age, country of residence and the date you apply, and they change. The official government page always wins.

Documents almost every country asks for

  • Valid passport (check how many months of validity are needed)
  • Offer or acceptance letter from the university
  • Academic certificates and transcripts (SSC, HSC or O/A Levels, bachelor's), with official English translations if needed
  • English test result (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL or another accepted test), unless the university or visa rules exempt you
  • Proof of funds: bank statements, education loan letter, or sponsor documents. Keep a clear record of where the money came from.
  • Sponsor documents if a parent pays: income proof, tax returns, relationship proof (birth certificate)
  • Passport-size photos to the country's rules
  • Statement of purpose or study plan, if asked
  • Any gap explanation (work experience letters, etc.)

United Kingdom (Student visa)

  • Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) number from your university
  • Proof you have held the required money for 28 consecutive days, unless you are exempt (GOV.UK lists who is)
  • Tuberculosis (TB) test certificate from a UK-approved clinic, if you will stay more than 6 months. Bangladesh is on the list of countries that need this.
  • Payment of the Immigration Health Surcharge and visa fee
  • ATAS certificate, only for some science and engineering subjects

Canada (Study permit)

  • Letter of acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)
  • Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL) for most applicants. IRCC lists some exceptions. IRCC's 2026 update added a new exception for master's and doctoral degree students at public DLIs. Please confirm your own case on the IRCC page before you apply.
  • Proof of financial support (IRCC sets the minimum amount and updates it)
  • Biometrics and, if asked, a medical exam by an IRCC panel physician
  • Quebec: a Quebec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ) if you study in Quebec

Australia (Student visa subclass 500)

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
  • Answers to the Genuine Student (GS) questions in the application
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the whole stay
  • Evidence of funds, if the Department asks for it (check the current amount)
  • Health examinations and biometrics, if requested

United States (F-1 visa)

  • Form I-20 from a SEVP-certified school
  • Receipt for the I-901 SEVIS fee
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • Visa fee receipt and interview appointment at the U.S. Embassy in Dhaka
  • Financial documents that match the amount on your I-20
  • Be ready to explain your study plan and your ties to Bangladesh at the interview

Tips that prevent problems

  • Make every document tell the same story: names, dates of birth and amounts must match across passport, certificates and bank papers.
  • Do not use fake or "arranged" documents. They can lead to a refusal and a ban.
  • Keep scanned copies of everything in one folder.
  • Book TB tests, biometrics and interviews early; slots in Dhaka can fill up.

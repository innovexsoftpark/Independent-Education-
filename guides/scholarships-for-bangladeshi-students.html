Scholarships for Bangladeshi students: what to apply for, and when

By the Independent Education counselling team · Updated 18 November 2026

Fully funded scholarships are competitive and their deadlines are early, often 8 to 12 months before the course starts. Knowing the calendar is half the work.

Dates change every year. The months below are when each scholarship usually opens, based on recent rounds. Always check the official website for this year's dates and eligibility before you prepare documents.

What fully funded scholarships usually cover

  • Tuition fees
  • A monthly living allowance
  • Return flights
  • Health insurance
  • Sometimes a language course or preparation year

How to build a strong application

  1. Start a year ahead. Collect certificates, transcripts and attestations early; some need apostille or ministry attestation in Bangladesh.
  2. Get your English test done. Many scholarships set a minimum IELTS or TOEFL score.
  3. Answer the scholarship's own questions. Do not reuse one generic essay; each scheme looks for different things (leadership, academic merit, development impact).
  4. Choose referees who know your work. Give them your CV and goals and enough time.
  5. Apply for several. Combine one or two fully funded schemes with university scholarships, which are easier to win.

Watch out for

  • Websites or agents asking for a fee to "guarantee" a scholarship. Official schemes never sell places.
  • Out-of-date dates copied from old posts. Check the official site.

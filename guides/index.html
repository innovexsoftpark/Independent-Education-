Study abroad guides

Free, practical guides from Independent Education for students in Bangladesh who are planning to study abroad. Each one links to official government, embassy or test-provider pages, because rules change.

Studying abroad involves a lot of decisions: which country, which course, how to pay for it, which English test to take and how to apply for a visa. These guides explain each step in plain language, with the figures and documents Bangladeshi students are usually asked for. Read them in order, or jump to the topic you need.

How these guides are written

  • Written for Bangladeshi students. The guides cover the countries, tests and documents students from Bangladesh ask about most.
  • Linked to official sources. Visa rules, fees and money requirements come from government, embassy, university or test-provider pages, and each guide lists its sources at the end.
  • Dated. Every guide shows the date it was last updated. Rules can change at any time, so always check the official page before you pay or apply.
  • Not legal advice. The guides give general information. For your own situation, talk to a counsellor or check with the embassy.

If you spot something out of date, please let us know and we will check it.

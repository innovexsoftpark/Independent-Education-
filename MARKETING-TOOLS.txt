INDEPENDENT EDUCATION - ADVANCED MARKETING TOOLS
================================================

Everything below is OFF until you paste an ID into IE_MARKETING near the
js/site.js (search for "const IE_MARKETING"). Tracking tools only
load after a visitor clicks "Accept" on the cookie notice.

Check each platform's current help pages when setting up: their menus and
rules change often, and the steps below may be out of date.

1. ENQUIRY CAPTURE (most important)
   LEAD_ENDPOINT: "/server-tracking/lead.php"
   - Every "Book counselling" form in the chat box is saved to a CSV file
     (opens in Excel / Google Sheets) and emailed to your office.
   - Settings: the 'leads' section of ie-tracking-config.php.
   - If the server fails, the visitor still gets the "Send on WhatsApp" button,
     so no enquiry is lost.
   - Spam protection: hidden trap field, 5 enquiries per IP per hour,
     only accepts requests from your own domain.
   - Many shared hosts block PHP mail(). If emails don't arrive, check the
     CSV file, and ask your host about SMTP.

2. GOOGLE ADS
   GOOGLE_ADS_ID: "AW-XXXXXXXXX"
   GOOGLE_ADS_LEAD_LABEL: the label of a "Submit lead form" conversion action.
   - Sends a conversion every time someone submits the enquiry form.
   - Enhanced conversions: the email/phone are hashed by Google's tag before
     sending. Turn on "Enhanced conversions for web" in the conversion settings.

3. TIKTOK ADS
   TIKTOK_PIXEL_ID: from TikTok Ads Manager > Assets > Events > Web events.
   - Sends PageView, ViewContent, Search, Contact and SubmitForm (lead).
   - Lead email/phone are SHA-256 hashed in the browser (advanced matching).

4. LINKEDIN ADS
   LINKEDIN_PARTNER_ID: Campaign Manager > Analyze > Insight Tag.
   LINKEDIN_LEAD_CONVERSION_ID: create a conversion with the "event-specific"
   (JavaScript) method and paste its ID.

5. MICROSOFT CLARITY (free heatmaps and session recordings)
   CLARITY_ID: from clarity.microsoft.com > your project > Settings.
   - Shows where people click, how far they scroll, and where they get stuck.
   - The enquiry forms are masked, so what visitors type is not recorded.

6. META (FACEBOOK / INSTAGRAM) ADVANCED MATCHING
   With SERVER_ENDPOINT on, leads now include hashed email, phone and name
   in the Conversions API event, which usually improves ad matching.
   Set ENHANCED_MATCHING:false to switch this off for every platform.

7. ENGAGEMENT EVENTS (GA4)
   scroll_depth (50% and 90%) and engaged_visit (60 seconds on the page).
   Mark engaged_visit as a key event in GA4 if you want to optimise for it.

8. REFERRAL LINKS
   Give students, agents or partners a personal link:
     https://www.YOUR-DOMAIN/?ref=nadia
   Enquiries from that visit show "Heard about us via: referral: nadia"
   (in WhatsApp messages, emails and the CSV file).
   Works together with UTM links, e.g.
     https://www.YOUR-DOMAIN/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=uk-jan-intake

9. EXIT-INTENT NUDGE
   A small "Planning to study abroad?" card appears once per visit, either
   when a desktop visitor moves to leave the page or after 45 seconds of
   reading. It opens the enquiry form. It never shows after an enquiry is sent.

Privacy: the privacy policy on the website already describes these tools.
If you add a tool not listed here, update the policy too.

Types of event

Study abroad expos

A large event where many universities, colleges and counsellors come together in one place. You can walk from desk to desk, compare countries and courses side by side, and pick up brochures and fee information.

In person · usually a few hours, drop in any time

More about study abroad exposGood for

Students who are still choosing a country, course or university, and parents who want to compare options together.

What to expect
  • Desks for universities from several countries
  • Talks on destinations, scholarships and visas
  • Free one-to-one advice from counsellors
  • Fee and scholarship information to take home
What to bring
  • Your SSC, HSC or degree results (copies)
  • A list of questions and your budget
Alert me about study abroad expos
Spot admission days

A day when a university's representative meets students in person or online and reviews their documents on the spot. If you meet the entry requirements, you may get a faster assessment of your application, and some universities can give a decision on the day.

In person or online · book a time slot in advance

More about spot admission daysGood for

Students who already know which university or course they want and have their documents ready.

What to expect
  • A short interview with the university representative
  • A check of your academic documents and English score
  • Advice on entry requirements, scholarships and next steps
  • A faster assessment, or sometimes a decision on the day
What to bring
  • Passport
  • Academic certificates, mark sheets and transcripts
  • English test result (IELTS, PTE or TOEFL), if you have one
  • CV and work experience letters, for postgraduate courses
Alert me about spot admission days
Information sessions

A focused talk on one destination, subject or step of the process, such as applying to UK universities, scholarships in Europe or preparing a Canadian study permit file, followed by questions.

In person at our office or online · usually 1 to 2 hours

More about information sessionsGood for

Students and parents who want clear, step-by-step information before they decide or apply.

What to expect
  • A presentation by a counsellor or a university representative
  • Up-to-date entry, cost and visa information
  • Time for your own questions
  • Handouts or checklists to take home
What to bring
  • Your questions
  • A notebook, or your phone for notes
Alert me about information sessions
IELTS workshops

A practical workshop on the IELTS test: how each section works, the common mistakes and how to manage your time, with practice tasks.

In person or online · usually 1 to 3 hours

More about IELTS workshopsGood for

Students who are about to take IELTS, or who need a higher score for their course or visa.

What to expect
  • An overview of the Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking sections
  • Practice tasks and tips for each section
  • Advice on the score your course needs
  • Guidance on booking your test and preparing for it
What to bring
  • Pen and paper
  • Your target score, if you know it
Alert me about IELTS workshops
Online webinars

An online session you join from home on your phone or computer. Topics include countries, universities, scholarships and visas, often with a university representative.

Online · usually 45 to 90 minutes · we send the joining link when you register

More about online webinarsGood for

Students outside Dhaka, or anyone who cannot come to the office.

What to expect
  • A live presentation
  • Questions answered in the chat
  • Links to useful resources afterwards
What to bring
  • A stable internet connection
  • Headphones, if you can

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register for an event?

Yes, please register for every event, even ones where you can drop in. Registering lets us keep a seat for you, tell the university representatives how many students to expect and send you any changes to the time or venue.

Registration matters most for spot admission days and online webinars. At a spot admission day, each student gets a time slot with the university, and slots can fill up. For a webinar, we can only send you the joining link after you register.

Each event listing says how to register. Use the Register button on the listing, or fill in the register your interest form on this page. A counsellor will confirm your place by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Are the events free?

Each event listing says whether the event is free. If a listing doesn't say, ask us before you register and we'll tell you.

Counselling with Independent Education is always free, so you can talk to a counsellor at an event or at our office without paying.

Who can attend?

Anyone who is thinking about studying abroad is welcome, at any stage of their plans. That includes:

  • Students who are studying for, or have finished, SSC, HSC, O levels or A levels and want to study a bachelor's degree or diploma abroad
  • Students who are studying for, or have finished, a bachelor's degree and want to study a master's degree
  • Working people who want to go back to study
  • Parents and guardians who want to understand the process and the costs

You don't need to have chosen a country or a course yet. Expos and information sessions are a good place to start if you're still deciding.

Can my parents come with me?

Yes. Parents and guardians are welcome at all in-person events and can join online webinars with you.

It often helps to bring them. They can ask the university representatives about tuition fees, living costs, scholarships and safety, and you can plan your budget and proof of funds together. If your parents or another family member will sponsor your studies, it is useful for them to hear what the bank documents need to show.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. Please ask your friend to register as well, so we have a seat and, at a spot admission day, a time slot for them too.

What should I bring to a spot admission day?

Bring everything the university needs to assess your application. A complete set of documents gives you the best chance of a decision on the day.

  • Academic documents: your SSC and HSC (or O level and A level) certificates and transcripts. For a master's degree, also bring your bachelor's degree certificate and transcripts.
  • Passport: your valid passport, or a copy of it.
  • English test result: your IELTS, PTE, TOEFL or Duolingo English Test result, if you have one.
  • CV: an up-to-date CV, especially for postgraduate courses or if you have work experience.
  • Other documents: a statement of purpose and recommendation letters, if you have them ready.

Bring the originals and a set of photocopies, and keep scanned copies on your phone. The event listing or your confirmation message will say if the university needs anything else.

Can I get an offer at a spot admission day?

Sometimes. If you meet the entry requirements and bring all your documents, some universities can assess your application on the day and give you a decision. Others review your documents at the event and send their decision later.

An offer may be conditional. For example, it may depend on your final results or an English test score. An offer letter also doesn't guarantee a student visa: the visa is a separate application, and we can help you with it.

I haven't taken IELTS yet. Can I still come?

Yes. You can still talk to universities and find out which English test scores they accept. Some universities can give you a conditional offer and ask you to send your test result later.

If you need to prepare for the test, our IELTS workshops and preparation can help, and you can book your test when you're ready.

Can I join an event online?

Yes, when the event is online. Online webinars, and some information sessions, show "Online" in the listing.

When you register, we send you the joining link by email or WhatsApp. You can join on your phone or computer. Use a stable internet connection, join a few minutes early and keep your questions ready. You can usually ask them in the chat.

What if I miss a webinar?

Some webinars may be recorded, and the listing or your joining message will say so. If you missed one, contact us. A counsellor can go over the topic with you or tell you when the next session is.

What if I register but can't come?

Please let us know as soon as you can by phone, WhatsApp or email, so we can give your place to another student. We can book you into a later event, or a counsellor can meet you at our office or online instead.

Where are in-person events held?

Unless the listing says otherwise, in-person events take place at our office: 89/2 Siraj Tower, New Airport Road, Kakoli, Banani, Dhaka 1213. Larger events, such as expos, may be held at another venue, and the listing will give the full address.

What happens after the event?

A counsellor will contact you to talk about what you learned and what you'd like to do next. There is no obligation to apply.

If you decide to go ahead, we can help you shortlist courses, prepare your documents, apply to universities, look for scholarships and apply for your student visa.

  • Use an Alert me button under the type of event you're interested in.
  • Fill in the register your interest form on this page.
  • Sign up for our scholarship and intake updates by email at the bottom of this page.
  • Follow Independent Education on social media.

FAQ

Common questions

Is counselling really free?

Yes. Your first consultation and ongoing counselling with Independent Education are free of charge. University application fees, test fees and visa fees are paid to those organisations directly.

When should I start my application?

Ideally several months before your chosen intake, because applications, offers and visas all take time. Contact us early and we'll build a timeline with you.

Do I need IELTS to study abroad?

Most universities ask for an English test result. Many accept IELTS, PTE, TOEFL or Duolingo, depending on the university and country. We'll tell you what your course accepts.

Can you help with my student visa?

Yes. We guide you on the documents you need, financial evidence and interview preparation. The final visa decision is always made by the destination country's immigration authority.

Which country is best for me?

It depends on your subject, budget, academic results and career plans. Book a free session and we'll compare your options together.

New scholarships, intake dates and events, sent by email about once a month. Unsubscribe any time. See the latest updates.
